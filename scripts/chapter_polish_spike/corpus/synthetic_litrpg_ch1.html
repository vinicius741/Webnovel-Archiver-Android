Chapter 1 — Arrival

The world ends on a Tuesday.

Not with fire. Not with a bang. Not even with a whimper, really.

Just a notice.


[SYSTEM NOTICE]
Integration will begin in 60 seconds.
All inhabitants will be assigned a Class.
Resistance is not possible. Compliance is not required.
Good luck.

Kai Mizrahui was holding a sandwich.

A pastrami sandwich. Rye bread. Mustard, no pickles.

He looked at the sandwich. He looked at the sky. He looked back at the sandwich.

"Sixty seconds," he said. "Great. Fantastic. Perfect."

The countdown hit zero.

Light everywhere. A lurch. A fall.

A landing.

Kai stood in a plaza of white stone. Ten thousand other humans stood with him, arranged in neat rows like a choir about to sing. Somewhere a bird sang. Somewhere a child cried. Somewhere a man was still, somehow, eating a sandwich.

Not Kai. His sandwich was gone.

"I want my sandwich back," he told nobody.

Nobody answered.


A screen unfolded in the air in front of him. Blue light. Black text. The font was, insultingly, Comic Sans.

NameKai Mizrahui
Class[Undetermined]
Level1
HP87/87
MP12/12
Strength4
Agility11
Willpower9

Four strength. Eleven agility. Nine willpower.

A laugh. A groan. A headache.

"Undetermined," he read aloud. "Even the System doesn't know what to do with me."

The girl next to him had a Class already. [Ice Sorceress], hers said, in elegant silver script. She caught him looking.

"What?" she said.

"Nice font," Kai said. "Mine's Comic Sans."

"That's the worst thing I've ever heard."

"It's not even in the top ten worst things today."

Her name, her screen said when she angled it his way, was Yun Sol. Level 1, like everyone. Ice Sorceress, unlike anyone within three rows of them.

[SYSTEM NOTICE]
Tutorial Zone 44 is active.
First trial begins in 10 minutes.
Survive.

Survive. Just that. One word. No instructions, no FAQ, no customer service.

The crowd murmured. The murmured rolled outward like a wave. Ten thousand voices doing the math at once, and the math was bad.

Yun Sol rolled her shoulders. "Then we should find the trial gate."

"We?" Kai said. "There's a we now?"

"You have Comic Sans. I have standards. Together we average out."

"That's not how averaging works."

"That's not how survival works alone."

A fair point. A cold point. A point with edges.

The trial gate was a slab of black stone at the plaza's edge, and it was already drinking people. One per second. A flicker. A gone.

Kai counted the disappearing. Forty-one. Forty-two. Forty-three.

"Do we run in or walk in?" he asked.

"Walk," Yun Sol said. "Running is for people with plans."

"I have a plan."

"Is the plan the sandwich?"

The plan was, in fact, the sandwich. He said nothing. She knew anyway. Ice Sorceress, apparently, came with perception.

Forty-four. Forty-five.

They walked in.
