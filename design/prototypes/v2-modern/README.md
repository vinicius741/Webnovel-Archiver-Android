# v2 · Modernized (Material 3 Expressive, dark)

**Concept.** Today’s information architecture is unchanged: one stack rooted at Library, with app-bar entry points to Audio generation, Downloads and Settings. What changes is the visual and interaction layer, rebuilt as an M3 Expressive app. It uses tonal surfaces, large shapes, bottom sheets for every menu, and colour taken from each story’s cover.

**Palette.** The app seed is warm gold-amber (`oklch(0.84 0.115 84)`) on warm neutral surfaces: `surface` 0.155 L, then container-lowest → highest at 0.125 / 0.185 / 0.205 / 0.235 / 0.27 L. Story-scoped screens (Details, Select, Trends, AI, Reader chrome, Now Playing, Chapter audio, mini player, sheets opened from them) get **dynamic colour**. `js/core.js` samples the cover onto a 24×36 canvas and builds an OKLCH hue histogram weighted by chroma. The winning hue drives primary, containers, the hero wash and lightly tinted surfaces. With no cover it falls back to `WNA.hue`. Chroma is floored, so grey covers still get a tint.

**Type.** Google Sans Flex (ROND axis on headings), falling back to Roboto Flex. Emphasized scale: 34/42 large titles, 28 hero titles, 22 section titles, 16/15 list titles. Icons are Material Symbols Rounded, subset with `icon_names` (about 43 KB). If you add an icon, add its name to the URL in `index.html`.

**Shapes and motion.** Cards and sheets use 24–28 px corners. Buttons are pills that morph to a squircle on press, and icon buttons morph from circle to squircle. The play button morphs from circle to rounded square while playing. Grouped lists are “segmented”: 24 px outer corners, 4–6 px inner corners, 2 px gaps. Transitions are shared-axis X between screens, a vertical container transition for Now Playing, and a cover → details hero flight. Springs are approximated with overshoot cubic-béziers.

**Key interactions.**
- **Library.** Large collapsing title, M3 search bar, sticky tabs with counts (swipe the list to change tab), and a scrollable chip row with Sort, sources, top tags and an “All tags” sheet. Long-press starts selection: a contextual bar plus a floating toolbar with Move and Delete, both with Undo. The extended FAB shrinks on scroll. The Continue listening card takes its colour from the story.
- **Details.** Hero with a blurred cover wash and badges. Connected stat tiles (score/followers → Trends). Coverage bar with a bookmark pin. Big “Continue · Ch. N” button and Listen. A **split button** for Download remaining ▾. Sync, and an EPUB split button. A live download banner with wavy progress. Patreon card, About with Read more, tag chips (tap = filter the library). The chapter list has search, filter chips and bookmark toggles.
- **Wavy progress.** Used for downloads, generation, Now Playing seek (wavy while playing, flat when paused) and the generation ring. The M3E morphing-shape loading indicator shows while busy.
- **Reader.** Immersive: the bars hide on scroll and a tap toggles them. Live sentence highlight auto-scrolls; a “Back to the voice” chip appears after you scroll away. Double-tap a paragraph to read from it. A floating chapter toolbar, or a docked player while listening.
- **Sheets.** Every menu, sort, filter, picker and confirmation is a modal bottom sheet. You can drag a sheet down to dismiss it. On wide screens sheets centre at a 640 px max width.

**Wide (≥ 840 px).** Details becomes list-detail (400 px info pane plus an independently scrolling chapter pane). The library uses 2-column cards or a 6-column cover grid. Audio generation splits into queue and settings columns. Now Playing puts cover and controls side by side.

**Porting notes (Compose).** All tokens map 1:1 to `MaterialTheme.colorScheme` roles. Build dynamic colour per story with `material-color-utilities` (`QuantizerCelebi` → `Score` → `SchemeTonalSpot`, dark) from the cover bitmap, cached by story id. Wrap story screens in a nested `MaterialTheme`. Components exist in Material 3 1.4+/Expressive: `LargeFlexibleTopAppBar`, `ExtendedFloatingActionButton(expanded=)`, `SplitButtonLayout`, `ButtonGroup`, `SegmentedButton`, `FilterChip`, `ModalBottomSheet`, `LinearWavyProgressIndicator` / `CircularWavyProgressIndicator`, `LoadingIndicator`, `HorizontalFloatingToolbar`, `SnackbarHost` with an action. Shape morphing on press is provided by `ButtonDefaults.shapes()`. Use `ListDetailPaneScaffold` for the wide Details layout and `SharedTransitionLayout` for the cover hero.

**Prototype-only hooks.** `#/library?view=cover`, `#/library?select=1`, `#/library?tag=LitRPG`, `#/reader/{id}/{ch}?play=1`, `#/player?play=1`, `#/narration/{id}?chapter={ch}`, `#/story/{id}/ai?tab=desc`.
