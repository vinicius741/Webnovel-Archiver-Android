Findings

P2-01 — “Download Remaining (16)” enqueued 30 queue items

Repro: Opened Arcane Zoologist: The Dragon’s Chosen at 9/25, tapped Sync Chapters, and waited for the details screen to settle at 23/39 with Download Remaining (16). Tapping that action eventually produced a correct 39/39 story, but Downloads showed Arcane Zoologist … 30/30 chapters and the persisted queue contained 30 completed rows (chapter indices 25–38 and 0–15).

Impact: The user-facing remaining count and the actual queue plan disagree. The final story is complete, but the app performs unnecessary/duplicate queue work and makes download progress/accounting difficult to trust after sync.

Suggested investigation: compare the chapter set used to render downloadedChapters/Download Remaining with the chapter set passed to the queue planner after a sync refresh. The persisted queue evidence is stronger than the visual count alone.

P2-02 — Read EPUB gives no visible result without an external EPUB handler

Repro: Generated arcane_zoologist_the_dragon_s_chosen_progfan_magic_Ch1-39.epub, confirmed the file existed and passed unzip -t, then tapped the enabled Read EPUB button. After waiting, the app remained on story details with no chooser, toast, or error message. Logcat showed a VIEW intent for application/epub+zip with no visible handoff on this emulator.

Impact: On a device without an EPUB-capable app, the action appears dead. A clear “install/select an EPUB reader” message or an in-app reader fallback would make the failure understandable.

This is environment-sensitive: the emulator had no external EPUB reader installed. The generated EPUB itself was valid.

P3-01 — Trends score chart repeats rounded Y-axis labels

The Trends screen displayed a score range of 4.34–4.36, but the chart’s five visible Y-axis labels rounded to 4.36, 4.36, 4.35, 4.34, 4.34. The line is usable, but duplicated labels make the scale look incorrect and reduce confidence in small changes.

Suggested improvement: choose a tick formatter/interval that guarantees strictly increasing displayed labels, or show more precision when the fitted range is narrow.

P3-02 — Horizontal Library/Add Story content is truncated on the QA viewport

On the 1080×2400 emulator, the Library filter chip row cuts off the third source chip and the trailing All tab is only partially visible. Add Story’s URL hint also ends at “SpaceBattl…” because the clipboard button consumes the remaining width. The controls remained usable through horizontal/edge interaction, but the initial presentation looks clipped rather than intentionally scrollable.

Suggested improvement: make the horizontal affordance explicit, give the chip/tab strip a visible scroll treatment, and shorten or ellipsize the Add Story hint intentionally.
