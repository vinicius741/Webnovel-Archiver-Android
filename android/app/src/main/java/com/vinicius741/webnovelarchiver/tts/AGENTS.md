# Text-to-speech and the player

Applies to `tts/` (including `kokoro/` and `phonon/`), `feature/player/`, and the Reader (`feature/reader/`, including its read-along `ReaderReadAlong` and `ReadAlongPlanning`). Generated chapter audio is in `narration/AGENTS.md`. These rules add to `android/AGENTS.md`.

- `feature/player/` is the podcast-style TTS player UI: the app-level mini-player bar (`TtsMiniPlayer`, app chrome the activity owns and attaches once, refreshed after every screen build through `onScreenBuilt`; docked in the app shell's bottom shelf above the navigation bar while a session is loaded, hidden on Reader, Now Playing and Listen; the Reader docks its own `ReaderPlayerDock` in place of chapter nav while that chapter is read aloud, with a "Back to current sentence" pill when the user scrolls away; the highlight, following and chapter auto-follow live in one per-page `feature/reader/ReaderReadAlong` controller, bound to the WebView's attachment, with rules in `ReadAlongPlanning` and a render-only page script that also pulls the state as a safety net) and the Now Playing screen (`showPlayer`, `AppRoute.Player`). Now Playing runs on `PlayerScreenModel`, which reads `PlayerData` (`RepositoryPlayerData`: playback, the playing story, its pack's voice, the chapter's sentences, the saved place) while a renderer collects. `PlayerRenderer` rebuilds only when the playing chapter changes and closes the screen on an authoritative stop. It patches the controls on each update and ticks the time labels once a second (`tickerFlow`, bound while attached). Transport, speed, sleep timer, history and stop go through `PlaybackControls` (`ServicePlaybackControls` sends the playback service's commands), which the Listen screen and the mini player share. Playback state comes from `TtsEngine.playbackState`; per-story resume positions persist in `tts_positions.json` (see `docs/tts/tts-podcast-player.md`). `TtsSessionStore` writes listener changes after a 250 ms debounce but plain forward progress at most every 10 s (`PROGRESS_INTERVAL_MS`, the most a process death loses); pause, stop and finish must keep writing at once.
- The Reader (`showReader`, `AppRoute.Reader`) runs on `ReaderScreenModel`, one per reader route, over `ReaderData` (`RepositoryReaderData`: the chapter prepared off the main thread by `ReaderDocumentPreparer`, the story, playback, display preferences, the listening place) and `ReaderPlayback` (`ServiceReaderPlayback`: the listen starts in `ListenStarts` and the playback service's commands). Its state is the page (`ReaderPage`: Loading, Ready with a `generation`, Missing, Failed), the bookmark, this chapter's playback slice (`playing`, seeded from the persisted session; the docked player shows while it is non-null) and the display (text size, "Pure black"); toasts and the "Continue listening" choice come out of `messages` and `readAloudChoices`. Opening a chapter (`showReader`, also Back to it) prepares it again behind a spinner; a re-render (a fold) builds at once from the prepared chapter. `ReaderRenderer` creates the WebView and its `ReaderReadAlong` with every build, patches the bookmark button and the dock from the model's state, and rebuilds only when the page's generation changes ("Pure black" reloads the chapter; a text size is applied to the live page by script and the model refreshes the prepared HTML so a later rebuild keeps it). Previous/Next open the nearest downloaded chapter in the story as it is now (`readableChapter`), and the settings sheet (`showReaderSettingsSheet`) acts on the reader shown when a change is made, since it outlives a chapter auto-advance. The page does not remember its scroll position across a rebuild (a fold, Back to it): it opens at the top unless read-along scrolls it to the spoken sentence.
- `TtsEngine` plays chapters only from generated packs (`PackTtsOutput`) and loads no speech engine or model. The Listening settings sample (`TtsSamplePreview`, through `TtsOutputs`) renders sentences to `cacheDir/tts_audio/` (`TtsAudioRenderer`, `TtsAudioCache`) with the user-chosen engine (`TtsSettings.enginePackage`): an Android TTS app or a built-in model (`tts/kokoro/BuiltInModels.kt`: Kokoro v1.0 through sherpa-onnx (Kokoro v0.19 is `retired`: not offered, its settings load as v1.0 via `TtsEnginePlanning.withoutRetiredEngine`, its packs still play and servers still run it), Phonon 31M through `tts/phonon/PhononTTS.kt`, JNA over `src/main/jniLibs/*/libptts_ffi.so` rebuilt by `scripts/phonon/build-android-lib.sh`; each downloaded on demand by its `KokoroModelInstaller` and loaded only while in use in its `KokoroRuntime`, both held by `BuiltInEngines` in `AppContainer`, on one thread per fast core (`KokoroThreads`; chapter generation on the phone runs Phonon two sentences at a time on phones with 6+ fast cores and memory to spare, `PhononCopies`); Phonon offers all 37 English catalog voices, listed identically in `BuiltInModels.kt` and `scripts/narration-server/setup.sh` (a test checks), and an older Phonon install downloads only voices added since; a built-in model needs no download when computers generate (live speech then falls back to the phone's engine, `TtsEnginePlanning.standIn`), and the Listening settings sample generates on the computers instead; story-description read-aloud was removed in November 2026 and an old saved description session is never resumed (`TtsSessionPlanning.isResumeEligible`); see `docs/tts/built-in-models.md`; Settings' Kokoro **Test speed** (`KokoroBenchmark`, UI in `feature/settings/SettingsKokoroBenchmark.kt`, debug builds only) times generation on the device and exports a shareable report; see `docs/tts/kokoro-built-in-voices.md`) and plays them as one stream (`TtsPcmPlayer`), falling back to live speech (`LiveTtsOutput`) if rendering fails; see `docs/tts/tts-audiobook-playback.md`. `TtsEngine` tracks its output as one `TtsOutputPhase` (Silent / Running), separate from the persisted pause, and reports playing only when the output is running and not paused. A pause is the listener's decision: only an explicit play or recovery from a brief interruption may start audio, so moves, restores and restarts must keep it. Saved places resolve by their text anchor (`TtsPositionPlanning.resolve`), so bump `TtsTextPreparation.CHUNKING_VERSION` when sentence numbering changes. Do not read repository settings on the main thread before `awaitRepositoryReady`: a restarted service can run ahead of startup, and the first read can deadlock against it.
